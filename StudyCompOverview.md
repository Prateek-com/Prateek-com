# 📘 StudyComp – Smart AI Study Companion (Work in Progress)

StudyComp is a smart web-based platform designed to help college students
manage their *study planning, attendance, and exams* efficiently.
The project focuses on solving one of the biggest problems faced by students:
**maintaining attendance above 75% while studying effectively.


🚧 Project Status: Frontend Completed 

## 🔥 Key Features:

### 1️⃣ AI Study Plans
Students can input:
- Their daily free hours
- Weak and strong subjects

Based on this input, StudyComp uses AI logic to generate a *personalized daily study schedule* optimized for:
- Better retention
- Balanced workload
- Focus on weak subjects.

- ### ⭐ 2️⃣ Smart Attendance System (Most Important Feature)

Attendance is one of the biggest challenges for college students, and
StudyComp is designed to *solve this problem visually and practically*.

#### How it works:
- First, students add all their subjects.
- When the student clicks on the *Attendance section*, all subjects are displayed.
- Clicking on a subject opens a *monthly calendar view*.
- The student can switch between months easily.


#### Attendance marking options:
- ✅ *Attended class* → Date turns *Green*
- ❌ *Absent / Not attended* → Date turns *Red*
- 🟡 *Official Leave / No class* → Date turns *Yellow*

- #### Smart Calculation:
- Attendance percentage is *automatically calculated* for each subject.
- The percentage is displayed next to every subject.
- Visual indicators help students instantly know whether they are *safe or at risk*.

- 🎯 This feature ensures students:
- Never fall below *75% attendance*
- Can visually track attendance month-wise
- Avoid last-minute attendance stress

- 🎯 Helps students maintain **75%+ attendance** without stress.

### 3️⃣ Exam Management
StudyComp also helps students manage exams by providing:
- Countdown to important exam dates
- Syllabus coverage tracking
- Space for revision notes

Everything related to exams is organized in one place to reduce confusion and anxiety.
